Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Retro Stack Attack ("the Game") is developed and published by Muonity. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you play the Game.

1. Personal Information

Retro Stack Attack does not require you to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

However, some third-party services used by the Game may automatically collect certain technical and usage information as described below.

2. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including optional rewarded advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or process information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, interactions with the Game or advertisements, and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and related purposes.

Depending on your location, you may be presented with privacy and consent options regarding advertising. These choices are managed using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

For more information about how Google handles information, please see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Optional Usage Analytics

Retro Stack Attack uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help understand how the Game is played and to improve gameplay, balance, stability, and future development.

Usage analytics are disabled by default. You can voluntarily enable anonymous usage analytics in the Game's Settings. You can disable this option again at any time.

When usage analytics are enabled, the Game may send information about interactions with the Game, such as game starts, game overs, gameplay events, use of game features, bonus usage, rewarded-ad rewards, purchases or purchase-related events, and other similar gameplay events.

Firebase may also process technical information and identifiers associated with an app installation or device. The Game does not intentionally send your name, email address, or other directly identifying personal information through its custom analytics events.

For more information about Google Analytics for Firebase and Google's privacy practices, please see: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. In-App Purchases

Retro Stack Attack offers optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. The Game does not receive or store your payment card or bank account details.

Purchase-related information may be processed as necessary to verify purchases, restore purchased content, prevent fraud, and provide purchased items or features.

5. Google Play Games Services

The Game may use Google Play Games Services for features such as leaderboards.

If you use these features, information related to your Google Play Games profile and gameplay activity may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's privacy policies and your Google Play Games privacy settings.

6. Data Security

Data transmitted by the third-party services used by the Game is protected using encryption in transit where supported by those services.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Retro Stack Attack is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Third-party services used by the Game may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

8. Third-Party Services

The Game uses services provided by third parties, including:

  • Google Play
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase

These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, for example when features or third-party services used by the Game change.

Any changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.
