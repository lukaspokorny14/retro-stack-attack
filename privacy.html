Privacy Policy for Retro Stack Attack

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Retro Stack Attack handles information when you use the game.

Developer

Retro Stack Attack is developed and published by Lukáš Pokorný.

Information collected by the game

Retro Stack Attack does not require users to create an account and the developer does not operate a database containing player profiles or personal information.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on the user's device. This locally stored information is used only to provide game functionality.

In-app purchases

Retro Stack Attack offers optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store payment card details or other payment credentials.

Information necessary to verify purchases, such as product identifiers and Google Play purchase tokens, may be transmitted securely to the developer's purchase verification service and to Google Play services for the purpose of validating purchases and preventing duplicate or fraudulent purchase delivery.

Third-party services

Retro Stack Attack uses Google Play services related to app distribution and in-app purchases. Google may process information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

Advertising

Retro Stack Attack does not contain third-party advertising.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell users' personal information. Information required for purchase processing and verification is exchanged only with services necessary to provide and verify Google Play purchases.

Data security

Network communication used for purchase verification is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections. The developer takes reasonable measures to protect information processed by the purchase verification service.

Data retention and deletion

The developer does not maintain user accounts or a persistent database of player profiles. The purchase verification service processes purchase information only as necessary to verify transactions and does not intentionally retain purchase tokens as a player database.

Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. Records relating to purchases may also be maintained by Google Play according to Google's own policies.

Children's privacy

Retro Stack Attack does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The game does not include user accounts, chat, social networking features, or third-party advertising.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact
