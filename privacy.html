Privacy Policy for Retro Stack Attack

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Retro Stack Attack handles information when you use the game.

Developer

Retro Stack Attack is developed and published by Lukáš Pokorný.

Information collected by the game

Retro Stack Attack does not require users to create an account and the developer does not operate a database containing player profiles or personal information.

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on the user's device. This locally stored information is used to provide game functionality.

Advertising

Retro Stack Attack may display optional rewarded advertisements provided through Google AdMob. Players may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes. This may include the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location; interactions with the app and advertisements; diagnostic information related to app and SDK performance; and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available.

Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. The availability and use of certain identifiers may depend on the user's device settings, privacy choices, and applicable law.

Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where required by applicable law, Retro Stack Attack uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy and advertising consent choices. Depending on the user's location and choices, advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited.

Where required, users are provided with options to review or change their privacy choices.

In-app purchases

Retro Stack Attack offers optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store payment card details or other payment credentials.

Information necessary to verify purchases, such as product identifiers and Google Play purchase tokens, may be transmitted securely to the developer's purchase verification service and to Google Play services for the purpose of validating purchases and preventing duplicate or fraudulent purchase delivery.

Third-party services

Retro Stack Attack uses Google services including Google Play services for app distribution and in-app purchases, and Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertising. Google's User Messaging Platform may also be used to manage privacy and consent choices. These services may process information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell users' personal information. Information may be transmitted to Google and related service providers where necessary to provide advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, app distribution, purchase processing, and purchase verification functionality.

Data security

Network communication used by the game and its integrated services is transmitted using encrypted connections where supported. The developer takes reasonable measures to protect information processed by the purchase verification service.

Data retention and deletion

The developer does not maintain user accounts or a persistent database of player profiles. The purchase verification service processes purchase information as necessary to verify transactions and does not intentionally retain purchase tokens as a player database.

Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. Information processed by Google services may be retained or deleted according to Google's policies and the user's applicable Google and device privacy controls.

Children's privacy

Retro Stack Attack does not require users to create an account and does not include chat or social networking features. The game uses Google services for optional rewarded advertising. Advertising and related data processing are subject to applicable privacy requirements and the privacy controls provided through the integrated Google services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
